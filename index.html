    Finance — OTIF & Deductions

    OTIF = on-time, in-full — the share of orders delivered on time and in full. Deductions are shown as $ and as % of sales, with the reason in the exceptions table (shortage, compliance, damaged / returns). Pull deduction detail by PO before disputing — do not dispute from the dashboard total alone.

    Distributor movement (UNFI / KeHE) is shipments out of the distributor, not register sales. Never mix it with Walmart / Target sell-through in the same total without labelling it.

    How each KPI is defined

    Definitions (one line each)
    • Total sales $ — net sales in the period, by channel, in the brand currency.
    • Units — units sold in the period.
    • Velocity (units/store/week) — units ÷ stores carrying the item ÷ weeks. For Amazon / DTC, shown as units per 100 orders.
    • Weeks of supply — inventory on hand ÷ average weekly units.
    • In-stock % — share of expected facings / listings in stock.
    • OTIF % — on-time, in-full orders ÷ all orders.
    • Deductions $ — retailer / distributor deductions in the period, by reason where available.
